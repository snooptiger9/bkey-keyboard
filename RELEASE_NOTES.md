### Release v318.0

- **Languages no longer mix:** Words you type in one language no longer show up as suggestions in another. This covers Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, English, Spanish and French, and applies to suggestions while typing, next-word predictions and auto-correct.
- **Right language from the start:** Suggestions now follow the keyboard's language as soon as it opens, even when Arabic isn't one of your languages.
- **Arabic and Urdu:** They share a script but are now fully separate. Urdu-only letters are never learned as Arabic words.
- **Words in more than one language:** A word you use in two languages (for example "hotel" in English and Spanish) is now suggested in both.
- **Switching language mid-word:** A word you started before switching language is no longer learned or auto-corrected in the new language.
- **Older learned words:** Words saved by earlier versions are checked once and moved to the right language automatically.
- **Training sentence:** The field in Settings now appears only when bkey is your current keyboard, and it accepts typing only. Each word is saved in the language you typed it in, Hindi and Urdu included, and your text stays when you pull down the notifications.
- **Text shortcuts:** Expanded shortcut text is no longer learned as typed words.
- **Dictionaries:** Removed foreign and uncertain words from the English, Spanish and French dictionaries, plus mixed-in words, misspellings and broken entries from Arabic, Urdu and Hindi.
- **Spelling suggestions:** Fixed a bug that stopped dictionary-based typo suggestions for English, Spanish, French and Hindi.
- **Email addresses:** They are now saved and suggested in every language, including Hindi and Urdu.
- **Emoji size:** The mark showing the default size now lines up correctly on wide screens and in right-to-left languages.
- **Swipe down for symbols:** Now plays the swipe sound instead of a key click.
