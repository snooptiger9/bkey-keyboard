### Release v303.0

- **Backup & restore:** Added a new screen that saves your learned words, next-word suggestions and word shortcuts into one JSON file, and restores them on a new phone. Restoring only adds to what you already have.
- **Auto theme:** Added a half-white, half-black theme that follows Android, using the white theme in light mode and Matte black in dark mode. It replaces the old "follow system" switch.
- **Long-press to capitalize:** Lowercase English keys now give the capital letter on long-press, and the number row appears only while Shift or Caps Lock is on.
- **Cleaner long-press menus:** Symbols already on the symbols page are no longer repeated, and Spanish accents show only on the Spanish keyboard.
- **Typing stats page:** Added a dedicated page with animated counters for learned words and words picked by swiping up.
- **Redesigned learned words:** New look with a counter card, a rounded search field, and word cards with usage counts and next-word chips.
- **Floating GIF search:** The search button and field now float over the GIF grid as theme-colored pills, with the KLIPY credit inside the pill and equal margins on both sides.
- **Smart clipboard phone dialer:** Copied phone numbers get a quick-action call chip that opens the dialer with the number filled in.
- **Firmer floating capsules:** The floating buttons in the clipboard and emoji screens are 1% less transparent in every theme.
- **Renamed to bkey:** The standard build is now simply "bkey" (previously "bkey Clean"), and its APK is named `bkey-v303.apk`.
