## What's new

### Backup & restore
**Back up and restore your words.** A new *Backup & restore* screen saves everything the keyboard learned (words, next-word suggestions) and your word shortcuts into one JSON file, and restores it on a new phone. Restoring only adds to what you already have; nothing is deleted.

### Themes
**Auto theme.** A new half-white, half-black color follows Android: the white theme in light mode and Matte black in dark mode, switching on its own. It replaces the old "follow system" switch.

### Typing
**Smarter long-press on the English keyboard.** Lowercase keys now give the capital letter on long-press, and the number row appears only while Shift or Caps Lock is on. Symbols that already live on the numbers/symbols page are no longer repeated, and Spanish accented letters show only on the Spanish keyboard.

**Typing stats.** A new *Typing stats* page shows how many words the keyboard has learned and how many you picked by swiping up, with animated counters.

**Redesigned learned words.** The learned words screen has a new look: a counter card, a rounded search field, and word cards with usage counts and next-word chips.

### GIFs
**Floating GIF search.** The search button floats over the GIF grid with a magnifier, and the search field above the keyboard is now a floating, theme-colored pill. The search placeholder reads "Search KLIPY". The KLIPY credit now sits inside the pill, so no bar covers the GIFs. The grid margins are now equal on both sides.

### Clipboard
**Quick dial for phone numbers.** When a copied item is a phone number, a small call button opens the dialer with the number filled in. It never places the call for you.

### Polish
**Slightly firmer floating buttons.** The floating capsules in the clipboard and emoji screens are 1% less transparent in every theme.
