### Release v319.0

- **New language: Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia):** Turn it on from Settings → Languages. It uses the standard QWERTY layout with its own clean dictionary. Auto-correct is limited for now because the dictionary is still small; it will grow in future updates. The clipboard and emoji panels, the settings pages and voice typing all work in Indonesian.
- **Languages stay separate:** Indonesian words never show up in the other languages, and the other languages' words never show up in Indonesian.
- **Space bar:** The language name is hidden when only one language is turned on.
- **Clipboard:** Long-press a clipped text to Expand it when it is cut off, and Collapse it again.
- **Undo after deleting:** The Undo card now shows only after you stop deleting, not while you are still deleting.
- **Pasted links and numbers:** They no longer show up as suggestions after pasting.
- **Settings:** Rotating or folding the phone keeps you on the same settings page instead of going back to the main page.
- **Settings home page:** The title spacing now matches the other pages on Android 15 and newer.

**bkey Plus only**

- **Indonesian in Plus:** The GIF panel is in Indonesian, and verification codes are now detected in Indonesian messages.
