# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 22 November 2026

bkey is a keyboard, so it handles what you type. This page explains what it does with that.

## What bkey collects

Nothing. bkey does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## Data kept on your device

- **Learned words, text shortcuts, clipboard items and typing stats** are saved only on your device, to give you better suggestions. You can delete them any time in the settings, and Incognito mode stops learning and clipboard saving completely.
- Passwords and links are never learned.
- You can export a backup of your learned words to a file. That file is created only when you ask, and stays where you save it.

## Permissions (standard bkey build)

- **Internet:** none. The standard build cannot connect to the internet.
- **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** used only by the optional voice-typing button, and only after you allow it and tap the mic. Speech recognition is done by Android's own speech-recognition service. bkey itself never records, stores or sends audio.
- **Vibration (`VIBRATE`):** for the optional key haptics.
- bkey does not request access to SMS, contacts, photos, storage, location or notifications.

## bkey Plus (separate build)

bkey Plus adds optional features that need extra permissions: GIF search (internet, through KLIPY), image pasting and screenshot suggestions (photos), optional verification-code suggestions (reading incoming notifications, processed on the device only) and an in-app updater (internet, GitHub). Plus is not distributed through the stores that carry the standard build.

## Children

bkey does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
