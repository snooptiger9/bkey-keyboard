bkey is a keyboard (IME) built around the swipe gestures of the old BlackBerry Keyboard - Classic app, brought to a modern Android keyboard with more customization.

Gestures:

* Swipe up on any suggestion to accept it instantly
* Swipe sideways across the letter row to delete the last whole word at once (direction configurable in settings)
* Swipe down over the keys for copy/paste or the symbols layout (your choice in settings)
* Press-and-hold and drag the space bar to move the cursor character by character

Other features:

* Full Arabic, English, Spanish, Hindi, French, Urdu and Indonesian support, each tailored separately
* Smart clipboard with pin, delete, and auto-delete
* Emoji panel, a persistent number row, custom themes, and key sounds

This build has no internet access and does not request SMS, notification, photo, or storage permissions. The only sensitive permission is the microphone, used only by the optional voice-typing button.
